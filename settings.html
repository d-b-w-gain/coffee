What coffee.lan controls

Smart plugs

Tapo and Kasa (TP-Link), Shelly and Tasmota plugs work directly. Tapo plugs need “Third-Party Compatibility” turned on in the Tapo app. A plug that measures power (like the Tapo P110) on the machine adds the power trace, measured warm-ups and maintenance numbers below.

    Grinder · Mazzer Mini

    Single dosing: load a dose with the cup under the chute and tap “I’ve loaded a dose” (the plug switches off so you can turn the grinder’s own switch on). Each grind then runs for the timed grind plus a little extra. The motor is rated 50 s on / 50 s off, so no run is longer than that and each is followed by an equal rest.

    Grind times

    Load a dose, press Time it, and press Stop the moment it runs empty. Half & half and decaf use the regular time until they have their own.

    Machine power

    Needs a plug that measures power on the machine.
    wattsheater threshold
    Pull a shot and watch the trace: the heater is the big step, the pump a smaller one on top. Set the thresholds between what you see.
    With a power-measuring plug, “ready” comes from the heater’s first cut-out, and the estimate becomes the typical measured warm-up.

    Maintenance

    Daily numbers from the plugs. After a few weeks, slow drifts get flagged: heater drawing less (ageing element), longer warm-ups (scale), pump working harder or longer (scale, choked jet, vane wear), grinder working harder (dull burrs, needs a power-measuring plug on the grinder).
